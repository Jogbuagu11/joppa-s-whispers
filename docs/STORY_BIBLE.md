# Whispers of Joppa — Story Bible

## 1. The world

**Joppa, around AD 40.** An old, rocky harbor on the Mediterranean coast, the port for Jerusalem.
Fishermen, tanners, potters, and traders. Rome rules from Caesarea, a day's journey north.
A small community of believers ("the Way") meets in homes. Everyone knows everyone,
and news travels fastest at the town well.

**Harbor lore (Silas tells these):**
- Jonah ran from God by booking a ship out of Joppa (Jonah 1:3). "Every runaway in Israel sails from our docks, one way or another."
- Solomon's cedars from Lebanon were floated down to Joppa for the Temple (2 Chronicles 2:16).
- Joppa's harbor is dangerous: a ridge of rocks offshore. Storms come fast. The harbor watchtower's beacon has been dark for two years.

**Period rules for writers:**
- No paper books, no glass windows in ordinary homes, no coins named after modern money.
- Esther's letters quote only the Hebrew Scriptures or sayings of Jesus that believers passed along. Paul's letters (Ephesians, Galatians) were not written yet, so they never appear in dialogue or letters.
- The Armor of God and Fruit of the Spirit chains are symbolic items on Esther's rooftop board. Characters never mention them by those names in dialogue.
- Scripture text: KJV (or WEB). Both are public domain.

## 2. The cast

### Naomi — protagonist, ~30
- **Who:** Esther's granddaughter, a skilled baker. Widow of Eliab, son of a wealthy merchant family in Caesarea.
- **The rumor she carries:** after Eliab died of fever, his mother Jael put it about that Naomi had been secretly visiting a Roman officer's household, implying unfaithfulness. Jael's family sent her away.
- **The truth (revealed Ch. 6):** Eliab was quietly a believer in the Way. Every week, he and Naomi sent bread to the household of Cornelius, a God-fearing centurion who fed the poor of Caesarea. Eliab's family despised Romans, so Eliab made Naomi promise to keep it secret. She kept the promise even after it ruined her.
- **Voice:** dry humor, guarded, quick. Softens slowly. Says "Hm." when she doesn't want to answer.
- **Arc:** "Call me Mara" (bitter, like Naomi in Ruth) → restored. She stops defending herself and starts defending others.
- **Look (for art doc):** dark curls tied back, flour on her sleeves, indigo headscarf, a carved olive-wood pendant from Eliab.

### Grandma Esther — deceased, guide through letters
- **Who:** Joppa's baker for 50 years and one of the first believers in town. Close friend of Tabitha. Died three months before the story.
- **Device:** she hid 12 letters, written on pottery shards (ostraca), around Joppa for Naomi to find.
- **Voice in letters:** warm, funny, direct, with baking metaphors. Signs off "— your Savta" (Grandma).

### Caleb — boat builder, ~35
- **Who:** widower, raising his daughter Joy. Builds and repairs boats at the docks.
- **The rumor:** his wife Leah drowned two years ago; the town says he sent her out in a badly built boat to deliver cargo for money.
- **The truth (Ch. 5):** Leah took the boat out herself in a storm to rescue a capsized fishing skiff. On the skiff was Tobiah, Demas's younger brother. Demas had refused to go out. Leah saved Tobiah and was lost on the rocks.
- **Voice:** few words, gentle, practical. Answers questions with work.
- **Arc:** frozen grief → builds a new boat named *Leah* → opens his heart (slow-burn, chaste romance with Naomi).
- **Look:** broad shoulders, sawdust, short beard, a leather tool apron, always a pencil-reed behind his ear.

### Joy — Caleb's daughter, 8
- **Who:** bold, curious, attaches herself to Naomi immediately. Wants honey cake. Repeats everything she hears at the well, which makes her a gossip mirror for the adults.
- **Voice:** blunt questions, big feelings. "Is it true you were sent away? Why? Were you bad?"
- **Look:** wild hair, a too-big tunic, bare feet, a wooden toy boat Caleb made her.

### Zilpah — queen of the well, ~60
- **Who:** widow, knows everyone's business, sweet with a sting. Main antagonist of Chapters 1–3.
- **Secret (Ch. 4):** at 19, she was betrothed. False rumors about her purity ended the betrothal and her prospects. Only Tabitha stood by her and made her new clothes when her family cast her out. Over the years, Zilpah became the thing that broke her.
- **Voice:** "I'm only saying what people are saying, dear." Compliments that wound.
- **Arc:** gossip → repentance when Tabitha dies and is raised → in Ch. 6 she refuses to spread a rumor about Naomi, which proves her change.
- **Look:** fine embroidered shawl (Tabitha's work, though nobody knows), many bangles, sharp eyes.

### Elder Amos — leader of the house church, ~70
- **Who:** kind, forgetful, stubborn. The community meets in his small home.
- **The rumor (Ch. 2):** the widows' fund, money collected to support Joppa's widows, is missing, and Amos is blamed.
- **The truth:** a creditor was about to take widow Hannah's two sons into debt servitude. Amos paid the debt from the fund and planned to repay it by selling his own small vineyard plot, but fell ill before he could tell anyone.
- **Voice:** slow, proverbs, "Well now." Laughs at himself.

### Marcus — Naomi's younger brother, ~26
- **Who:** left home after their father's death years ago. Now collects harbor tolls for a Roman-contracted tax farmer. Despised by everyone.
- **The rumor (Ch. 3):** he cheats fishermen and informs on them to Rome.
- **The truth:** he records smaller catches than the real ones and pays the difference out of his own wages, to protect the fishermen. He is nearly ruined and sleeping in a boat shed.
- **Voice:** sarcastic armor over shame. Calls Naomi "Sister" when angry, "Nomi" when he isn't.
- **Arc:** prodigal returns → tells the truth to the chief collector, loses his position → becomes a crewman, joins the believers. One of the "six brothers" who go with Peter to Caesarea (Acts 11:12).

### Demas — marketplace storyteller, ~40
- **Who:** entertains the market with tales and news from up and down the coast. Charming and funny. Spreads rumors because a good story draws a crowd.
- **Secret (Ch. 5):** he refused to go out in the storm to save his own brother Tobiah. Leah went instead and died. His guilt became the story he told about Caleb.
- **Arc:** confession at the house church → uses his gift to tell true stories (he becomes the town's teller of what God did in Joppa).
- **Voice:** theatrical, "Gather close, friends, gather close…"

### Silas — old fisherman, ~75
- **Who:** Esther's oldest friend. Comic relief and the town's memory. Tells stories slowly, with detours.
- **Voice:** "Now, that reminds me of the year the fish swam backward…" Calls Naomi "little loaf."
- **Role:** introduces the board in the tutorial; remembers the night of the storm (Ch. 5).

### Hannah — widow (minor, Ch. 2)
Quiet, frightened, two teenage sons (Eli and Ben). Avoids the well because she's ashamed.

### Jael — Naomi's mother-in-law (Ch. 6)
Proud Caesarea merchant's widow. Started the rumor about Naomi. Arrives in Joppa in Ch. 6 after learning the truth from Cornelius's household.

### Lucius — servant of Cornelius's household (Ch. 6)
One of the two household servants Cornelius sends to Joppa (Acts 10:7). Recognizes Naomi as "the bread woman" who delivered every week.

### Biblical figures (stay consistent with Acts 9–11)
- **Tabitha (Dorcas):** a disciple "full of good works," who made tunics and garments for the widows (Acts 9:36–39). She falls ill and dies; Peter raises her (Acts 9:40–41).
- **Peter:** comes from Lydda when sent for (Acts 9:38). Stays "many days" with Simon the tanner (Acts 9:43). Has his rooftop vision at Joppa (Acts 10:9–16). Goes to Cornelius in Caesarea with brothers from Joppa (Acts 10:23). Is criticized for eating with Gentiles (Acts 11:2–3).
- **Simon the tanner:** his house is by the sea (Acts 10:6). Tanning was considered an unclean trade, so the town whispers about Peter staying there.
- **Cornelius:** a devout, God-fearing centurion of the Italian band who gave alms and prayed (Acts 10:1–2). Appears through his servants and as reported news.

**Rule:** Peter and Tabitha say only things consistent with Acts. Miracles happen exactly as written, nothing added. Their scenes are reverent and short. Peter's key lines come from scripture (KJV/WEB).

## 3. Relationship map
- Naomi ↔ Esther (grandmother, through letters)
- Naomi ↔ Marcus (siblings, estranged → reconciled)
- Naomi ↔ Caleb (slow-burn, chaste)
- Naomi ↔ Joy (instant bond; Joy pushes the romance along without knowing it)
- Zilpah ↔ Tabitha (Tabitha saved her 40 years ago)
- Demas ↔ Tobiah (brothers; Demas's guilt)
- Caleb ↔ Demas (Demas's rumor destroyed Caleb's name)
- Esther ↔ Silas (lifelong friends)
- Naomi ↔ Jael (mother-in-law; source of Naomi's rumor)

## 4. Chapter breakdowns

Each beat = 2–4 story tasks (≈30–40 tasks per chapter). **[S: X]** marks a spiritual order
tied to the beat. **[L]** marks where a letter is found. Every chapter ends on a cliffhanger.

### Chapter 1 — Homecoming
*Location: Esther's bakehouse · Unlocks: Bakery, Fruit of the Spirit · Crown: Imperishable (after Ch. 2)*
1. Dusk at the harbor. Dock workers whisper, "That's the widow they sent away from Caesarea." Silas greets her: "Little loaf. You came."
2. The bakehouse is boarded up. Naomi clears the doorway (tutorial task).
3. On Esther's rooftop, Silas shows her Esther's "prayer corner" (the board). **[L1]** in the oven niche. **[S: Love — Naomi]**
4. Joy sneaks in. "Is it true you were sent away? Were you bad?" Naomi: "Hm." Joy wants a honey cake.
5. The oven is relit. First bread for the fishermen. Silas brags about it all over the docks.
6. Zilpah visits with sweet questions about Caesarea. **[S: Patience — Naomi]**
7. In the market, Demas tells a tale about "a widow from Caesarea with Roman friends." Customers stop coming.
8. Caleb silently repairs the bakehouse shutters and refuses payment. Joy explains her mother died "in the sea."
9. Naomi bakes for the harbor anyway, free. **[S: Kindness — Naomi]** **[L2]** behind a loose brick.
10. The bakehouse is fully restored. A few customers return.
11. **Cliffhanger:** Elder Amos stumbles in, pale. The widows' fund is gone, and the town already has a name for the thief: Amos.

### Chapter 2 — The Collection
*Location: the town well · Unlocks: House Church · Crown: Imperishable*
1. Morning at the well: everyone whispering about Amos. Zilpah presides.
2. The well's stone lip is cracked and the rope is rotten. Naomi starts restoring it (the irony isn't lost on her).
3. Amos falls ill with fever and won't explain the money. **[S: Peace — Amos]**
4. The believers stop meeting. Naomi gathers lamps and benches to keep them together. *(House Church chain unlocks.)*
5. Zilpah pushes for Amos to step down. **[L3]** under the well's bucket stone.
6. Naomi notices widow Hannah avoids the well and draws water at dawn alone.
7. Joy reports that Hannah's boys said "the elder saved us."
8. Naomi visits Hannah gently. Hannah confesses that a creditor came to take her sons, and she begged Amos for help. **[S: Faithfulness — Hannah]**
9. Amos recovers. He shows the bill of sale for his vineyard plot, already arranged to repay the fund.
10. At the restored well, Hannah speaks publicly. Zilpah is silent. Amos forgives everyone who doubted him. **[L4]** (the anchor verse).
11. **Cliffhanger:** a man in a fine cloak with a Roman toll seal walks onto the docks. Naomi drops her bread basket. It's Marcus.

### Chapter 3 — The Tax Collector
*Location: the fishing docks · Unlocks: Armor of God · Crown: Rejoicing*
1. Marcus collects tolls. Fishermen spit at his feet. Naomi pretends not to know him.
2. Flashback in dialogue: after their father died, Marcus left. Esther wrote to him for years; he never answered. **[L5]**
3. The docks are rotting. Naomi organizes repairs with the fishermen, who refuse to work if Marcus is near.
4. Demas spreads it around: Marcus is informing to Rome about unpaid tolls.
5. Silas, unusually serious, asks Naomi to keep her brother away from his crew. **[S: Shield of Faith — Marcus]** *(Armor of God unlocks.)*
6. Naomi finds a page from Marcus's ledger blown into the harbor. It has two columns of numbers that don't match.
7. She confronts him in the boat shed where he's sleeping. The truth: he records smaller catches and pays the difference himself.
8. The chief collector arrives from Caesarea to audit. Marcus will be exposed and ruined. **[S: Belt of Truth — Marcus]**
9. Marcus chooses to tell the chief collector the truth himself. He loses his position.
10. The fishermen, hearing what he did, take him on as a crewman on Silas's boat. Silas: "Every runaway sails from Joppa. Some sail home." **[L6]**
11. **Cliffhanger:** a cry from the upper street. Tabitha has collapsed.

### Chapter 4 — Tabitha
*Location: Simon the tanner's house · Unlocks: Tabitha's Loom · Crown: Righteousness*
1. Tabitha is ill. Naomi brings bread. Tabitha asks her to finish the tunics for the widows. *(Tabitha's Loom unlocks.)*
2. Tabitha dies (Acts 9:37). The town grieves. Handle quietly and briefly.
3. The widows gather in the upper room, weeping, holding the garments Tabitha made (Acts 9:39). **[L7]**
4. Zilpah is absent. Naomi finds her alone at Tabitha's empty loom.
5. Zilpah's secret: 40 years ago, a rumor destroyed her betrothal, and only Tabitha stood by her and clothed her. "I became the thing that broke me." **[S: Gentleness — Zilpah]**
6. Two men are sent to Lydda to bring Peter (Acts 9:38). Caleb and Marcus go.
7. Peter arrives. He sends everyone out, kneels, prays, and says "Tabitha, arise" (Acts 9:40). She opens her eyes. Keep the scene short and reverent.
8. Joppa is stunned, and many believe (Acts 9:42).
9. Zilpah publicly asks forgiveness of the people she gossiped about, starting with Naomi. **[S: Self-Control — Zilpah]**
10. Peter lodges with Simon the tanner (Acts 9:43). The town whispers about a holy man staying in an unclean trade's house. Naomi helps restore Simon's house to welcome him. **[L8]**
11. **Cliffhanger:** Joy asks Peter if he can bring her mother back. Caleb walks out without a word. Across the street, Demas looks away.

### Chapter 5 — Caleb's Boat
*Location: the house church · Unlocks: Anointing Oil · Crown: Glory*
1. Caleb stops working. His boatyard goes quiet.
2. The believers have outgrown Amos's home, so the town starts building a proper house for the church. *(Anointing Oil unlocks.)*
3. Demas, to a crowd, retells the old story: Caleb sent Leah out in a leaky boat for money.
4. Joy hears it at the well and asks Naomi, "Did Papa kill Mama?" **[S: Love — Joy]**
5. Naomi asks Silas. He remembers the storm night slowly and painfully.
6. Silas's memory: Leah took the boat herself, rowing out toward a capsized skiff. **[L9]**
7. Naomi finds out who was on the skiff: Tobiah, Demas's brother, who survived.
8. Tobiah tells her Demas refused to go out. Leah went in his place.
9. Naomi speaks to Demas privately, not to shame him. Demas breaks. **[S: Goodness — Demas]**
10. At the house church dedication, Demas confesses publicly. Caleb forgives him. Amos anoints the new building with oil. Caleb begins a new boat, named *Leah*. **[S: Horn of Oil — Caleb]** **[L10]**
11. **Cliffhanger:** noon. Peter is on Simon's rooftop. Men from Caesarea knock at the gate below, and one of them stares at Naomi in recognition.

### Chapter 6 — The Gentile's Table
*Location: the harbor watchtower · Unlocks: The Word · Crown: Life*
1. Peter tells the believers about his vision on the rooftop (Acts 10:9–16). Naomi bakes for the visitors from Caesarea.
2. Lucius, Cornelius's servant, recognizes her: "The bread woman!" The old Caesarea rumor arrives in Joppa.
3. Demas's old crowd wants the story. Zilpah refuses to repeat it. "I've told my last story about another woman." **[S: Faithfulness — Zilpah]**
4. Peter leaves for Caesarea with six brothers from Joppa (Acts 10:23, 11:12), including Caleb and Marcus. Naomi stays behind.
5. The believers split. Some say Peter is defiling himself by going into a Gentile's house.
6. Naomi leads the restoration of the harbor watchtower, dark since the night Leah died. *(The Word unlocks.)* **[L11]**
7. The brothers return. The Holy Spirit came upon Cornelius's household, and they were baptized (Acts 10:44–48).
8. Lucius returns with a gift from Cornelius, and the truth: Naomi and Eliab fed Caesarea's poor through Cornelius's household, and Eliab was a believer.
9. Jael arrives in Joppa, humbled. Naomi chooses to forgive her. **[S: Full Armor of God — Naomi]**
10. The Gentile's Table: believers of every background eat at one long table on the harbor. Naomi bakes the feast.
11. The watchtower beacon is relit. **[L12]**
12. **Season cliffhanger:** in the final letter, Esther reveals that Naomi has a sister, Miriam, who was taken to Tyre as a child. "She is alive. Go and find her."

## 5. Esther's letters (KJV)

**Letter 1 — The oven niche (Ch. 1)**
> My Naomi. If you are reading this, you came home, and I am already Home. Don't cry on the dough — it makes it salty. I know what they said about you in Caesarea. I never believed a word. Fear will tell you to hide in this kitchen. Don't. *"There is no fear in love; but perfect love casteth out fear."* (1 John 4:18) Open the shutters. — your Savta

**Letter 2 — Behind the loose brick (Ch. 1)**
> Every morning for fifty years I lit that oven in the dark, not knowing if anyone would buy a single loaf. Someone always came. *"It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness."* (Lamentations 3:22–23) The well remembers everything, little one. Go and listen. — your Savta

**Letter 3 — Under the bucket stone (Ch. 2)**
> Half this town has decided a man is guilty before breakfast. I have done it too, God forgive me. *"He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him."* (Proverbs 18:13) Hear the whole matter, Naomi. Especially from the ones who aren't talking. — your Savta

**Letter 4 — In the well's foundation (Ch. 2)**
> I carved this one for the well because it is the loudest place in Joppa. *"A froward man soweth strife: and a whisperer separateth chief friends."* (Proverbs 16:28) A rumor is a crack in a jar. It doesn't break all at once. It just leaks until nothing is left. Mend what you can. — your Savta

**Letter 5 — Addressed to Marcus, never sent (Ch. 3)**
> Marcus. I wrote you every month. You never answered, and I never stopped. When you come back — and you will — know this: *"But when he was yet a great way off, his father saw him, and had compassion, and ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him."* (Luke 15:20) Nobody has to earn their way back to this table. — your Savta

**Letter 6 — In Silas's net box (Ch. 3)**
> For both my grandchildren, who are stubborn like their grandfather. When the whole harbor is against you, remember who stands with you. *"The LORD is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower."* (Psalm 18:2) — your Savta

**Letter 7 — In Tabitha's sewing basket (Ch. 4)**
> Tabitha and I made a pact: whoever went first would leave something for the other's girl. If you are reading this, she found you. Grief is not a lack of faith, Naomi. *"Weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning."* (Psalm 30:5) Wait for the morning. — your Savta

**Letter 8 — Under Simon the tanner's doorstep (Ch. 4)**
> People hold their noses at this house. Holy things happen in the places people hold their noses at. The Lord came *"to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness."* (Isaiah 61:3) Tabitha sewed that verse into everything she ever made. — your Savta

**Letter 9 — Carved into Caleb's old boat (Ch. 5)**
> I saw Leah go out that night. I have never told anyone, because nobody asked me, and the town preferred its story. The Lord said, *"Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends."* (John 15:13) She did. Don't let them bury her twice. — your Savta

**Letter 10 — In the house church cornerstone (Ch. 5)**
> The sea took a great deal from this town. It does not get the last word. *"When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee."* (Isaiah 43:2) Build anyway. Love anyway. Bake anyway. — your Savta

**Letter 11 — In the watchtower stairwell (Ch. 6)**
> Someday the person who hurt you most will stand at your door. You will want to shut it. I did, once, and I regretted it for thirty years. The Lord said, *"For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you."* (Matthew 6:14) Open the door, Naomi. — your Savta

**Letter 12 — In the beacon's oil basin (Ch. 6 finale)**
> *"For mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people."* (Isaiah 56:7) I always believed the Lord's table would be long enough for everyone. Now, the hardest thing. You had a sister. Miriam. She was taken to Tyre when you were too small to remember, and your mother made me swear never to speak of it. She is alive. Go and find her. — your Savta

## 6. Season 2 (outline)
- **Setting:** Tyre, the great Phoenician port to the north.
- **Hook:** Naomi searches for Miriam. Caleb, Joy, and Marcus come along; Silas sends them off with a story.
- **Theme:** gossip across cultures, and identity. Who are you when your name is a rumor in a city that doesn't know you?

## 7. Dialogue rules
- Max 140 characters per line.
- Every scene: 4–12 lines.
- Each character speaks in their own voice (Section 2).
- Rumors are always shown being spread (Demas, the well, Zilpah) before they're disproven, so players feel the pull of gossip and then the cost.
- Never resolve a rumor with revenge, humiliation, or a "gotcha." Truth comes out, someone repents, someone forgives.
- Lines marked **REVIEW:** go to the theological reviewer before shipping.
